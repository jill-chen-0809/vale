TECHNOLOGY & ADVANTAGES

Key Features & Engineering Benefits

Overcoming the traditional tradeoff between beam diffusion and central luminous intensity loss.

High Central Illuminance

Maintains high center lux by minimizing unwanted wide-angle scattering, preserving punchy long-throw illumination.

Clear Cutoff Boundary

Features controlled edge spread with rapid beam decay, ensuring sharp illumination cutoffs and zero peripheral glare.

Smooth & Controlled Beam

Precision micro-structures homogenize light to eliminate LED package shadows, yellow halos, and harsh hot spots.

Narrow Angles (1° to 15°)

Fine-tuned angular gradations from ultra-narrow to medium-narrow diffusion for precision optical fixtures.
